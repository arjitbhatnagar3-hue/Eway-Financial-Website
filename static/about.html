Our Story

Built on Long Relationships

Incorporated on 20 March 2009, EWAY Financial Consultancy Private Limited is a private company limited by shares. Our practice is built on long relationships, clear communication and practical solutions.

We work with businesses at every stage — helping them incorporate, stay compliant, manage their money well and access credit when they need it most.

  • Local expertise — serving Bareilly, UP and clients nationwide
  • Confidential, personalised advisory for every client
  • End-to-end support: advisory, compliance, taxation & credit
